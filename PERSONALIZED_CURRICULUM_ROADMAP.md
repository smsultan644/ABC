# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This document defines your complete 6-month personalized trading curriculum. It is built around one core principle: **survival first, consistency second, scale only after evidence**. The curriculum progresses through three phases — Foundations, Strategy Development, and Demo Execution — before any live capital is committed. Each phase has explicit graduation criteria. No phase is skipped. No shortcuts are taken.

**You will not be permitted to trade live money until you demonstrate competency across knowledge, risk management, technical analysis, strategy execution, statistics, psychology, discipline, and execution.**

The curriculum follows the **Show–Do–Test–Apply** cycle:

```
LEARN (theory) → OBSERVE (charts) → PRACTICE (paper/exercises)
→ TEST (quiz) → DEMO (apply) → REVIEW (analyze) → ADVANCE
```

You do not advance to the next module until you demonstrate competency in the current one.

# PART VIII — RISK MANAGEMENT FRAMEWORK (Capital Protection System)

## 8.1 Core Position Sizing Formula

```
Position Size (lots) = Account Risk ($) ÷ (Stop Loss in Pips × Pip Value per Lot)
```

**Example:**
- Account: $1,000
- Risk: 0.5% = $5
- Stop Loss: 20 pips
- Pip value (EUR/USD standard lot): $10

Position Size = $5 ÷ (20 × $10) = 0.025 lots (round to 0.02 or 0.03)

## 8.2 Daily Loss Limit Protocol

| Trigger | Action |
|---|---|
| Daily loss reaches 1% | Stop trading for the day. Review what happened. |
| Daily loss reaches 2% | Stop trading for 48 hours minimum. Mandatory reset. |

## 8.3 Weekly Loss Limit Protocol

## 8.5 Hard Prohibitions

**NEVER:**

- Use grid strategies without predefined exit
- Move stop loss farther away to avoid taking a loss
- Increase position size after a losing streak
- Trade during major news events (first 90 days)
- Trade more than 3 times in a single day
- Hold more than 2 simultaneous positions (early stages)
- Risk more than 0.5% per trade in Phase 1-3
- Trade when psychologically compromised (tired, emotional, distracted)
- Trade to "win back" recent losses

# PART X — BANGLADESH-SPECIFIC CONSIDERATIONS

## 10.1 Regulatory Status Summary

**Current factual position (as of 2026-08-19, sources cited below):**

1. **Forex/CFD trading through offshore brokers exists in a legal grey area in Bangladesh.**
   - Sources: ForexBrokers.com (2026), CompareBroker.io (2026), Bangladeshi legal analyses
   
2. **The Foreign Exchange Regulation Act (FERA) 1947** restricts capital outflows for speculative purposes. Remitting money from Bangladeshi banks for retail forex trading is potentially a FERA violation.

3. **BSEC (Bangladesh Securities and Exchange Commission)** has issued warnings against offshore brokers and has blacklisted several platforms.

4. **Most Bangladeshi traders fund offshore brokers through informal channels:**
   - bKash / Nagad (mobile money)
   - Skrill / Neteller / e-wallets
   - Cryptocurrency P2P transfers

5. **Exness entity for Bangladesh clients:** Likely **Exness (SC) Ltd** regulated by FSA Seychelles (not CySEC, FCA, or ASIC).
   - **No investor compensation scheme applies to Bangladesh clients.**
   - International regulatory protections do NOT cover Bangladesh accounts.

## 10.2 Required Pre-Live Actions

Before any live trading, you MUST:
1. ✅ Consult a Bangladeshi legal professional about FERA implications
2. ✅ Consult a Bangladeshi tax professional about income reporting obligations
3. ✅ Verify your funding method's legality with your bank/financial institution
4. ✅ Understand that no domestic recourse exists if broker disputes arise
5. ✅ Accept full personal responsibility for regulatory compliance
